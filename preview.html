Dear Aashish Prasad,

The Software Development Club, VIT Bhopal University, is pleased to appoint you as Event Management Co-Lead for the Session 2026—27. The appointment takes effect from the date of this letter and continues until the close of the session.

Working alongside your Lead, you are charged with the following:

Planning, execution and monitoring of the club's events and projects.
Mentorship of core members within your team.
Liaison between the team and the club's leadership.
Upholding the club's standards of conduct at university forums.

The office is held in trust for the members of the club. The Club is confident that you will discharge it with judgement and care.

Please acknowledge receipt of this letter in writing.

Dr. Praveen Lalwani
Faculty Coordinator
VIT Bhopal University

Dear Devyanshu Negi,

On the recommendation of the leadership, the Software Development Club is pleased to promote you to Technical Lead with effect from the date of this letter. The promotion is made in recognition of the work you have delivered over the preceding term.

Your charge is enlarged as follows:

Direction of the Technical domain and its roadmap for the session.
Mentorship of Co-Leads and core members, including review of their work.
Representation of the team's plan before the President and the coordinator.
Orderly handover of the duties you held previously.

All other terms of your appointment continue unchanged for the remainder of the Session 2026—27.

Please acknowledge receipt of this letter in writing.

Position

Effective

Dear Lorem Ipsum Dolor,

Following review by the leadership, the Software Development Club has decided to terminate your appointment as Design Co-Lead with effect from the date of this letter. The decision was taken after the matter was put to you and your response was considered.

The following applies from the effective date:

You cease to hold office and to represent the club in any capacity.
Club accounts, files, assets and credentials are to be handed over within seven days.
Work completed before this date remains credited to you in the club's record.
You may appeal in writing to the faculty coordinator within seven days.

Your membership of the university community is unaffected by this letter, and the club thanks you for the time you gave it.

Please acknowledge receipt of this letter in writing.

Dr. Praveen Lalwani
